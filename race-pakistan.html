RACE Pakistan x KGAS STEM

RACE Pakistan: High-Speed STEM & Motorsport Engineering

Designing, building, and racing high-performance miniature Formula 1-style cars.

  • Explore modules
  • View awards

Overview

RACE Pakistan is a joint initiative by NUVEX and the NUST Formula Student Team (NFST). It combines CAD/CFD engineering, aerodynamics, manufacturing, enterprise, and track racing.

  • 7-month Season
  • 1,000+ Participants
  • F1 in Schools Competition model
  • Two pillars Engineering and business

Modules

  • Mechanical / Aerospace

    CAD & Aerodynamics

    3D CAD, CFD, and drag reduction for miniature Formula-style cars.

  • CNC and 3D printing

    Manufacturing & Fabrication

    Precision machining, weight limits, and automated/CO2 launch systems.

  • Business strategy

    Enterprise & Marketing

    Sponsorship, branding, budgets, and market portfolios.

  • Communications

    Pit Display & Presentations

    Verbal judging, trade stand, and engineering portfolios.

  • Dynamics

    Track Performance

    Reaction times, top speed, launch physics, and race days.

KGAS involvement

The KGAS STEM Society takes part in RACE Pakistan. Teams include the roles Team Principal, Lead Engineer, Resource Manager, and Designers.
