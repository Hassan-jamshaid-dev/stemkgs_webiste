Privacy policy

How this static site relates to your privacy.

This STEM Society website is a static informational site. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not include forms that send personal details to STEM Society through these pages.

  • We do not knowingly collect names, emails, phone numbers, or other personal data through this website.
  • There is no membership login, newsletter signup, or comment system on these pages.
  • Your browser may still send ordinary technical information to whichever host serves the files (for example, an IP address and page request in server logs). That behaviour is controlled by the host, not by scripts on this site that store profiles.
  • Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts when you open a page. That request is subject to Google’s own practices for font delivery.
  • If a future version of the site adds forms, analytics, or embeds, this policy should be updated before those features go live.

For privacy questions about STEM Society activities offline (events, team lists, and school records), speak with the society or the school through their usual channels — not through an invented address on this page.
