ACSEC — Aitchison College Science & Engineering Concept

Pakistan's Premier High-School STEM Olympiad

ACSEC is the flagship annual science and engineering competition hosted by Aitchison College in Lahore, Pakistan. Bringing together over 1,000 delegates from top schools nationwide, ACSEC tests theoretical knowledge, practical research, problem-solving, and build-engineering across high-stakes STEM disciplines.

  • Host Aitchison College Science Society (ACSS)
  • Location Lahore, Pakistan
  • Delegates 1,000+ nationally
  • Core focus Pure Sciences, Engineering, Robotics, Bio-Math and Cyber-Logic

Modules

Six competition tracks spanning theory, build, and applied STEM.

Robinson Challenge & Research Prize

Pure Physics & Theoretical Research

  • Advanced Newtonian mechanics and wave dynamics
  • Theoretical paper analysis and peer-review modeling
  • Scientific inquiry and hypothesis-testing under time constraints

Icarus & Aeronautical Build

Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering

  • Aerodynamics, lift, drag, and thrust
  • On-site model engineering and payload optimization
  • Flight performance testing under changing conditions

Asimo's Ascension

Robotics, Automation & Electrical Engineering

  • Microcontroller logic (Arduino/Raspberry Pi fundamentals)
  • Obstacle navigation and sensor integration
  • Real-time circuit troubleshooting and robot maneuverability

Fibonacci's Trials & Cosmic Corona

Applied Mathematics & Astronomy

  • Applied calculus, sequence modeling, and game theory
  • Observational astronomy, orbital mechanics, and space science logic
  • Data interpretation from cosmic telemetry models

Turing's Lair & Cryptic Realm

Computer Science, Logic & Cryptography

  • Cipher breaking
  • Algorithmic logic puzzles and recursion
  • Cybersecurity scenarios and competitive code debugging

Genetic Enigma & Forensic Science

Biology, Biochemistry & Forensic Investigation

  • Molecular biology, genetics, and DNA profiling
  • Forensic crime scene analysis and biochemical trace analysis
  • Epidemiological outbreak tracking and biological system analysis

The KGS STEM Society at ACSEC

The Karachi Grammar School STEM Society represents KGS at national STEM events and sends teams to compete across theoretical and engineering categories at ACSEC.
