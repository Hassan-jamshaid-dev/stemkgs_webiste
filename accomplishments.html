Inside KGS

Accomplishments

Programmes the society runs — recorded here only from what is already known.

STEM Society’s year is shaped by contests it designs and hosts. The notes below are short on purpose: no invented winners, dates, or awards — only the programmes already established in the society’s work.

  1. Build

    Hackathon

    Timed software work under a fixed brief.

  2. Contest

    Interhouse Science Bowl

    House teams in National Science Bowl format, O Level and A Level.

  3. Select

    STEM Gauntlet

    Internal assessments that form olympiad squads.

  4. Flagship

    Karachi Grammar STEM Olympiad (KGSO)

    The society’s flagship olympiad — twelfth edition — open to internal and external teams.
