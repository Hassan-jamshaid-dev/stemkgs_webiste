# STEM Society website — architecture

Source of truth for site structure and intent going forward.  
**Not all of this is built yet:** subject cards and competition cards are still summaries on the home page until each gets its own page.

Do not commit unless the user explicitly asks.

---

## Stack

- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- No backend
- Informational site (same approach as the previous website)

---

## Home page

**Nav:** About · Subjects · Competitions · Gallery · Accomplishments · Contact

- **Header:** STEM Society photos as the background; large bold STEM Society title on the left
- **Subjects:** 8 cards for the 8 modules. Each card is meant to hold, later:
  - pictures of that year’s team
  - a short account of the subject and its modules in KGSO
  - maybe accomplishments of its team members
- **Eight subjects already on the site:** Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Robotics, Biology, Astronomy, Crime
- **Internal competitions:** KGSO, Interhouse Science Bowl, STEM Gauntlet, Hackathon  
  Each is its own clickable card that leads to a page about the event, memorable pictures from it, and maybe last year’s winners
- **External competitions:** same structure as internal — ACSEC, Aero Pakistan, Race Pakistan, SciFi, SciNova, and any others added later
- **About** section
- **Contact** section
