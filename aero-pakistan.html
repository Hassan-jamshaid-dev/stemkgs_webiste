Designing, testing, and flying high-performance aircraft models, gliders, and flight systems.

Overview

Aero Pakistan is a national high-school and collegiate aeronautical engineering competition. It focuses on fixed-wing gliders, RC aircraft, propulsion, aerodynamics, and payload challenges.

  • Focus Flight mechanics and payload systems
  • Scale National high-school and collegiate division
  • Pillars Aerodynamics, glider fabrication, structural integrity, flight telemetry

Engineering modules

  • Mechanical and aeronautical engineering

    RC Aircraft & Fixed-Wing Design

    Airfoil selection, wing loading, center of gravity, thrust-to-weight.

  • Structural design

    Glider Payload Challenge

    Maximum payload, lightweight materials, structural stress resistance.

  • Electrical engineering and signals

    Flight Telemetry & Avionics

    Flight controller programming, altimeters, gyroscopes, telemetry logging.

  • Computational aerodynamics

    CAD Airframe Simulation

    3D CAD, CFD airflow, drag reduction.

  • Practical aviation

    Flight Testing & Pilot Performance

    Stability, obstacle navigation, precision landing, flight telemetry.

FAQ

What is Aero Pakistan?

Aero Pakistan is a national high-school and collegiate aeronautical engineering competition. It focuses on fixed-wing gliders, RC aircraft, propulsion, aerodynamics, and payload challenges.

What do teams work on?

Engineering modules cover RC aircraft and fixed-wing design, the glider payload challenge, flight telemetry and avionics, CAD airframe simulation, and flight testing and pilot performance.

Do delegates build their own aircraft from scratch?

Teams craft custom balsa and foam airframes for the competition.

How does the KGS STEM Society prepare for Aero Pakistan?

The KGS STEM Society prepares members with wind tunnel testing, CAD modeling, and custom balsa and foam airframes for Aero Pakistan.
