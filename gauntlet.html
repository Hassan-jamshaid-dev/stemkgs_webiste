About the STEM Gauntlet

The STEM Gauntlet is a highly competitive selection process designed to recruit the final team members for each subject’s official council for the academic year. Designed for candidates who were not selected during the initial interview phase, the Gauntlet offers a secondary pathway to secure a council spot.

The examination papers are known for being exceptionally challenging. They are authored and vetted directly by the current subject council team members—who themselves secured their roles through the rigorous interview process—ensuring that only the most resilient and analytically sharp minds earn a place on the council.
