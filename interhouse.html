About the event

The Inter-House Science Event challenges teams from Frere, Napier, Streeton, and Papworth through fast-paced quiz rounds, analytical problem-solving modules, and rapid-fire trivia in STEM subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Math). Students collaborate under tight time constraints to earn points for their overall House Shield.
