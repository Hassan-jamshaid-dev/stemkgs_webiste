PsiFi

A multi-day science and technology olympiad at LUMS.

PsiFi is a multi-day science and technology olympiad for school students across Pakistan, organised by SPADES (Society for the Promotion and Development of Engineering and Sciences) at LUMS, Lahore. It is open to O-Level, A-Level, Matric, and FSc students. Teams are 3–5 people.

It mixes academic challenges, engineering builds, and a final Showdown where top teams compete against each other. Winning a module is different from winning the Showdown, and both are different from the overall delegation title.

The next edition is PsiFi XVIII, 23–26 December 2026, at LUMS, Lahore. It is upcoming as of October 2026. Official PsiFi page.

Categories

A category is described only where the format is confirmed. Others are names only.

  • Scifinity

    Science-fiction and fandom. Structured character arguments, fictional mysteries, prop or storyline design, and explaining the science behind fiction. It is not only a film quiz.

  • Math Gauge

    Mathematics and applied problem-solving.

  • Robowars

    Robotics, design, control, and strategy. PsiFi XVII included Sumowars with custom-built robots.

  • Siege

    Engineering under constraints. In 2025, a boat-building round and a catapult challenge.

  • RC Planes

    Aircraft construction and aerodynamics. In 2025, designing and flying functional aircraft through laps.

  • Pixel Spectrum

    Creative, media, and technology. In 2025, an interactive scavenger hunt on the LUMS campus.

  • Kepler's Paradigm

  • Diagnosis Dilemma

    Medical and diagnostic problem-solving. Exact PsiFi XVIII tasks are not confirmed beyond that.

  • Carson's Lab

  • Science Crime Busters (SCB)

    Scientific investigation and deduction. In 2025, designed crime scenes and analytical challenges.

  • Nightlock Bloom

  • Ballad of Songbirds

  • Aerie of Mockingjays

  • Techwars

    A technology category from earlier editions. The format has varied.
