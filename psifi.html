PsiFi

A multi-day science and technology olympiad for school students across Pakistan.

Organised by SPADES (Society for the Promotion and Development of Engineering and Sciences) at LUMS, Lahore.

Official PsiFi page

Categories PsiFi XVII PsiFi XVI PsiFi X
  • Open to O-Level, A-Level, Matric, and FSc
  • Teams 3–5 people
  • Next edition PsiFi XVIII, 23–26 December 2026, at LUMS, Lahore
  • Status Upcoming as of October 2026

It mixes academic challenges, engineering builds, and a final Showdown where top teams compete against each other.

Winning a module is different from winning the Showdown, and both are different from the overall delegation title.

Categories

A category is described only where the format is confirmed. Others are names only.

Scifinity

Science-fiction and fandom.

What Scifinity includes

Structured character arguments, fictional mysteries, prop or storyline design, and explaining the science behind fiction. It is not only a film quiz.

Math Gauge

Mathematics and applied problem-solving.

Robowars

Robotics, design, control, and strategy.

PsiFi XVII Sumowars

PsiFi XVII included Sumowars with custom-built robots.

Siege

Engineering under constraints.

2025 Siege rounds

In 2025, a boat-building round and a catapult challenge.

RC Planes

Aircraft construction and aerodynamics.

2025 aircraft task

In 2025, designing and flying functional aircraft through laps.

Pixel Spectrum

Creative, media, and technology.

2025 Pixel Spectrum task

In 2025, an interactive scavenger hunt on the LUMS campus.

Diagnosis Dilemma

Medical and diagnostic problem-solving.

What is confirmed

Exact PsiFi XVIII tasks are not confirmed beyond that.

Science Crime Busters (SCB)

Scientific investigation and deduction.

2025 Science Crime Busters

In 2025, designed crime scenes and analytical challenges.

Techwars

A technology category from earlier editions.
